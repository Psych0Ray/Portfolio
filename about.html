HI THERE

I am a designer from Pune, currently studying at MIT Institute of Design, Pune.

I have always been fascinated by people. Our habits, contradictions, instincts, and the strange things that make us who we are.

I approach design through that curiosity. I like observing before assuming, questioning before solving, and looking for the human story behind a problem. The interface is only what we see; what interests me is everything underneath it, the motivations, emotions, behaviours, and moments that shape how we experience the world.

For me, design is less about having all the answers and more about being curious enough to keep asking better questions.
