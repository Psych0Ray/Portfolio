RELIQUE

CourseSemiotics and semantics
My roleDesign Direction, Research, Image and Story Generation
ToolsFigma, FigJam
Year2025

Relique is a museum website where artifacts tell their own stories, in the first person. Rooted in semiotics and semantics, it turns static collections into something you can wander: find a museum on the map, open its collection, and let an object's My Story play out in sound and image.
