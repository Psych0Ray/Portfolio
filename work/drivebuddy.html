DRIVE BUDDY

A sensor that plugs into any car's OBD-II port, and a companion app that turns each driving lesson into scores and specific feedback.

TypeUI design and user testing
ForDriving schools and their learners
MethodsThink-aloud tests, pre and post questionnaires
Year2024

EVERY MISTAKE, TIMESTAMPED.

DriveBuddy records acceleration, braking, clutch and gear changes during a lesson, then shows the learner exactly where it went wrong, like brakes not pressed at an obstacle, with a manual and a video to fix it.

4Skills scored every lesson: acceleration, braking, clutch and gears, confidence
6Users in think-aloud usability tests
3Tasks tested: book a lesson, review past lessons, check progress
