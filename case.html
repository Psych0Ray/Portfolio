Sonder health

A care platform rebuilt around the twenty seconds a nurse actually has. Shipped as a design system, not a screen.

RoleUI design, design system, research support
TimelineJan to Apr 2026, fourteen weeks
TeamTwo designers, four engineers
ToolsFigma, After Effects, Framer

Slide 01

The problem was never the screen.

Nurses were not struggling to read the interface. They were struggling to reach it: three taps deep, on a device held in one hand, in a corridor, mid-handover. Every design decision after this point came from watching that happen.
