Personal Armour
of Emperor Akbar
Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj Vastu Sangrahalaya 1581 CE Mumbai, India.

Step into the legacy of the Mughal Empire at CSMVS, Mumbai, and explore the grandeur of Emperor Akbar’s personal armor, a masterpiece of 16th-century craftsmanship. This exquisite armor, including a gold-adorned helmet, steel cuirass with Persian inscriptions, and arm guards featuring delicate koftgari work, showcases the fusion of Persian, Central Asian, and Indian influences in Mughal weaponry. Learn about Akbar’s direct involvement in designing this armor, documented in the Ain-i-Akbari, which highlights its symbolism of divine protection and intellectual depth. Experience this rare artifact of military and artistic brilliance through interactive exhibits at CSMVS and step into the rich history of the Mughal Empire.
