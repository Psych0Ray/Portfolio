MIDC, the Maharashtra Industrial Development Corporation, runs the state’s industrial estates. Its Integrated Command and Control Centre (ICCC) at Taloja watches the whole estate on a single dashboard.

  • 950+industries
  • 900hectares
  • 1000+connected devices
  • 24/7monitoring

But the dashboard only raised the alarm.

Cameras, smart poles, street lights, sensors and water meters all report to it. Checking an alert, sending a field team and keeping the record all happened by hand, over WhatsApp and a shared sheet filled in at the end of each shift.

We rebuilt it around what operators do.

See an alert, check it, send someone, close it. Every screen shortens one of those steps.

Everything after the alarm now has a place.

Escalating, assigning and messaging used to happen outside the dashboard. Now each has its own screen.
