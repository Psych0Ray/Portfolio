Most of Canva’s 180 million monthly users aren’t designers. They can tell when a poster looks wrong, but not why, and they can’t put it in design words.

Canva AI took them literally.

We evaluated Canva AI, Canva’s built-in assistant, with 50 prompts written the way these users write. It did what was typed, not what was meant: a new file instead of an edit, bold text for “more intense”.

We turned what we saw into seven tests.

Each failure gave its name to a quality the assistant needed. Together they spell CAPABLE, and every screen of the redesign answers to them.

  1. C

    Contextual understanding

  2. A

    Agency and control

  3. P

    Proactive suggestion

  4. A

    Aesthetic alignment

  5. B

    Boundary awareness

  6. L

    Learning and evolution

  7. E

    Efficiency and flow

We redesigned it as Orb, a design consultant.

Orb is our redesign of Canva AI. It sits beside your design, works in the file you have open, and asks before it changes anything. Step through one conversation with it.
